02 / THE REALITY

THAILAND IS NOT THE EASY OPTION.

And that's exactly why preparation matters. An entirely different football culture. Limited places for overseas players. Real competition for every contract. For the right player, the reward can be extraordinary — professionally and personally.

Our job isn't to persuade everyone to move. It's to help the right people make the right move, for the right reasons.

STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Before you
make the move.

We prefer honest conversations to big promises. These are some of the questions worth asking early.

Can you guarantee me a contract or trial in Thailand?

No. Thai football is competitive, and clubs control their own recruitment decisions. We never guarantee a trial, club response, offer or contract. Any regulated introductions, negotiations or representation must be undertaken by an appropriately licensed agent.

What level do I need to have played?

There is no blanket threshold that fits every position or club. Your age, current level, match footage, tactical profile and relevant league rules all matter. We assess those factors before recommending a route, and we may advise you not to pursue Thailand yet.

Can I apply if I'm not currently a professional player?

You can request an initial assessment, but overseas roster opportunities are limited and competition is strong. A serious track record and recent evidence of your standard are usually important. A positive assessment does not constitute club interest.

Will you help with visas, contracts and work permits?

We provide educational guidance and planning checklists on common requirements. Requirements differ by nationality, role, club and immigration circumstances. Formal immigration or legal advice must come from appropriately qualified professionals. We cannot issue visas or work permits.

Do I need to be in Thailand to begin?

No. Initial consultations, footage review and professional profiling can begin remotely. Travel should only be considered after an informed assessment of the likely football and financial realities.

How does this differ from a football agent?

Our initial services focus on readiness assessment, video analysis, player positioning and career-pathway education. Where a player's process moves into football-agent services, those activities must be performed through a properly licensed agent and an appropriate representation agreement.
